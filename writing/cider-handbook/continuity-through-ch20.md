## Ending and voice

The book closes by returning to the notebook and apple tree. Its promise is better questions and more precise French, not mastery of every cider method. The orchard route and bought-juice route remain equally legitimate learning paths. Local horticultural advice and current manufacturer instructions remain necessary for their particular applications.

If revising or extending, preserve vous/on, B1–B2 French, warm observational detail and restrained jokes. Keep sensory impressions distinct from analysis, and metaphors distinct from ingredients or operating instructions. Food pairings are possibilities to compare, not universal rules.
