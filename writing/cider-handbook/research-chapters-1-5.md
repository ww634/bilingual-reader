# Research record — chapters 1–5

Checked 20 October 2026. This record supports the opening chapters, not the future fermentation or packaging procedures. Examples, comparisons and notebook suggestions are original teaching material, not reports of an actual trial. No source passages are quoted in the chapters.

## Cider and sensory distinctions

- [Penn State Extension: Beer, Hard Cider, and Distilled Spirits Production](https://extension.psu.edu/food-safety-and-quality/beer-hard-cider-and-distilled-spirits/production). Relevant sections: Brewing Hard Cider and fermentation overview. Retrieved through indexed page text after direct access returned 403. Supports the basic conversion of sugars to ethanol and carbon dioxide and the distinction between sweetness and carbonation. Publication date not displayed. No fixed fermentation duration or treatment recipe adopted.
- [University of Wisconsin Extension: Cider Apples](https://cropsandsoils.extension.wisc.edu/cider-apples/). Crop Profile, especially juice composition and blending. Publication date not displayed. Supports fermented-apple-juice terminology and the roles of different fruit qualities. The text does not adopt this page's broad craft/industrial division or US regulatory terminology.
- [Washington State University: Cultivar Performance Database](https://cider.wsu.edu/ciderweb/). Introductory methods and classification notes; data collected 2002–2017. Indexed text reviewed after direct access returned 403. Supports the caveat that classification and performance depend on growing conditions. No cultivar recommendation, threshold or yield calculation copied into the chapters.
- [WSU: Hard Cider Production, hosted by UC Cooperative Extension](https://cesonoma.ucanr.edu/files/209991.pdf). Indexed passage on tannins, bitterness and astringency, page 6. Publication date not established from the retrieved excerpt. Supports distinguishing taste from mouthfeel. No chemical treatment or numerical recommendation used.
- [WSU: On Solid Ground—Warm Fuzzy, Guava, Cider](https://news.cahnrs.wsu.edu/article/wsus-on-solid-ground-warm-fuzzy-guava-cider/). Indexed section on tannin analysis. Supports the mouth-drying description of astringency. Used only for this narrow sensory statement.
- [NIDCD: Taste Disorders](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/taste-disorders). “How does your sense of taste work?”, updated 31 July 2023. Supports the distinction between taste qualities, texture and aromas released during chewing. No medical recommendations reproduced.

## Trees and orchard care

- [RHS: How to Grow Apples](https://www.rhs.org.uk/fruit/apples/grow-your-own). Choosing, plant care, harvesting and storage sections. Publication date not displayed. General horticultural reference, interpreted for a temperate northern-hemisphere setting. No calendar dates or universal spacings prescribed.
- [RHS: Rootstocks](https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/beginners-guide/fruit-basics/positioning-fruit/rootstocks). Rootstock function, vigour and site. Publication date not displayed. Supports the explanation of a grafted tree. Numerical size lists were not adopted.
- [RHS: Flowering Times of Apples](https://www.rhs.org.uk/Advice/PDFs/Beginners-Guide/ApplePollinationGroups.pdf). Indexed pollination-group guidance. Supports the need for overlapping flowering and compatibility. The book directs readers to local, variety-specific advice rather than listing combinations.
- [University of Minnesota Extension: Growing Apples in the Home Garden](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/growing-apples). Rootstock considerations, care and harvesting sections. Authors include Marissa Schuh and Madeline Wimmer; publication date not displayed. Supports dwarf-tree support requirements, the distinction between tree size and fruit size, and using several maturity indicators. Minnesota dates and cultivar lists are not generalised.
- [RHS: Apples and Pears—Pruning Made Easy](https://www.rhs.org.uk/fruit/apples/pruning-made-easy). Suitable forms, winter pruning, regrowth and avoiding indiscriminate cuts. Publication date not displayed. Used for principles, not an abbreviated technical pruning procedure.
- [RHS: Apples and Pears—Summer Pruning](https://www.rhs.org.uk/fruit/apples/summer-pruning). Suitable forms and timing. Publication date not displayed. Distinguishes restricted trained forms from free-standing trees. No cut lengths or shoot counts adopted.
- [RHS: Fruit Thinning](https://www.rhs.org.uk/fruit/fruit-trees/thinning). Natural early-summer drop, crop load, branch damage and biennial bearing. Publication date not displayed. No dessert-apple spacing rule presented as a cider-orchard rule.

## Harvest and fruit condition

- [MSU Extension: Checking Apple Maturity—What to Look For](https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/checking_apple_maturity_what_to_look_for). Indexed extract on starch-iodine testing reviewed; direct page inaccessible. Supports the existence and purpose of the starch test, not a recipe. Chapter 5 gives no mixing instructions, thresholds or variety-specific harvest index. The seed-colour discussion remains a qualified secondary indicator, never a maturity guarantee.
- [FDA: What You Need to Know About Juice Safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-juice-safety). Home preparation and untreated juice sections. Supports washing under running water, rejecting rotten fruit and recognising that appearance cannot establish safety. No claim that washing sterilises fruit.
- [FDA: Mycotoxins](https://www.fda.gov/food/natural-toxins-food/mycotoxins). Patulin section. Supports the mould/patulin connection. The book does not claim that fermentation never reduces patulin: it says not to rely on fermentation to repair poor fruit selection.
- [University of Maine Cooperative Extension: Bulletin 4191, Safe Home Made Cider](https://extension.umaine.edu/publications/4191e/). Growing/harvesting and storing apples, plus exclusion of spoiled fruit. Jim Schupp; revised by Jason Bolton; copyright 2008, 2011. Independent corroboration of fruit-condition precautions. This source concerns fresh, unfermented US “cider”: its preservation, heat-treatment and regulatory instructions are not applied to fermented cider. The book's first-batch route deliberately uses sound tree-picked fruit rather than claiming all traditional windfall practices are equivalent.

## Decisions and remaining gates

Supported for publication: basic cider overview; qualitative sensory distinctions; selecting a tree with local advice; orchard observation and qualified care principles; multiple ripeness indicators; careful handling, sorting and storage.

Excluded: named cultivar blend, universal maturity threshold, prescribed storage duration, chemical doses, actual first-batch measurements, yield assumptions, precise fermentation timetable and bottling instructions. These still require their chapter-specific research.

The opening pressure note is a consequence of the verified production of carbon dioxide: do not start fermentation in a sealed bottle. It is not a carbonation procedure. Later finishing chapters retain the independent-source and complete-procedure requirements in `technical-research.md`.
