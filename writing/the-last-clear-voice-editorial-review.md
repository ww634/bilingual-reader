Existing strong comic exchanges remain. Plain language carries the most painful material. The English is revised with the French, including imagery, and learner chunks are rebuilt and checked.

## Whole-published-manuscript audit

- **Progression:** the phone-box search remains fallible; Ruth's voluntary withdrawal corrects the disappearance hypothesis. Evidence advances through the blue folder, old documents, complete audio, the independent examination and Claire's letter. No new alibi or culpable act was added by this edit.
- **Fair play:** distinguish sound from proposed words, witness agreement from independent corroboration, appointments from proved meetings, payment instructions from receipt, and memory from a contemporaneous document. Keep the early and later forms of the same clue available to readers. Daniel's self-protective attention is visible rather than concealed from them.
- **Voices:** Daniel qualifies, corrects and sometimes hides in accuracy; Ben is practical, direct and able to answer back; Shah and Helen press on different responsibilities; Judith resists tidy explanations; Paul makes himself useful; Colin retreats into what he can certify. Do not flatten these into a shared supply of witty remarks.
- **Pacing:** retain quiet meals and ordinary work because they establish trust, context and the cost of its loss. Do not turn every ending into a threat or every technical explanation into a lecture. Chapters 19–21 escalate through recognition, confrontation and accountable action.
- **Continuity:** the dates, loaned objects, access permissions, source histories and chapter-22 handoff remain those in the continuity files. The new metaphors are narration, not fresh observations on which to base a solution.
- **Series layer:** the repeated limited-opinion phrase and Paul's forwarded generation question remain small, plausible details. Do not add sinister interpretation or identify an adviser in these chapters.
