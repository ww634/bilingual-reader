# The Open Door

Daniel stopped pulling on his arm. Every movement made Paul tighten his grip a little more. The edge of the bench dug into his ribs.

“You're hurting me.”

Paul pulled back the sheet with his free hand. He was looking for a pen.

To Daniel's right, a metal tray held spare parts. He pushed it to the edge, then into empty air. The crash filled the workshop. The small parts went on bouncing after the tray.

Paul turned his head. His grip loosened. Daniel pulled his arm back against his body and went round the end of the bench. He did not see where the paper had fallen. He could see the courtyard door.

“Daniel!”

Ben was in the doorway, his telephone to his ear. He pushed the door further, without coming in.

“Come on. This way.”

Daniel took two steps. Something pulled at his sleeve. Paul was behind him. Daniel braced himself against the door frame with his right shoulder. Ben caught the front of his cardigan and helped him across the threshold. The sleeve slipped out of Paul's hand.

They backed away into the courtyard. Ben was speaking into the telephone.

“We're outside. He's still there. At the back of the house.”

Paul put one foot on the threshold. Daniel heard a voice coming from the passage.

“Don't move. Show your hands.”

A police officer was approaching along the side of the house. Another guided Daniel and Ben back towards the gate. Daniel tried to tell him that his telephone was still inside. He needed above all to keep walking.

At the gate, Ben stopped. His bag was on the ground, against the wall. He left it there.

From the street, Daniel could no longer see anything but the top of the door. He was asked to sit down. He had his left hand against his chest. He could not keep it still.

An officer came back to tell them that Paul had been restrained and arrested. No one was to go back into the workshop.

Ben looked at Daniel.

“Did he speak to you about my mother?”

“No.”

Ben lowered his eyes. He was still holding the telephone to his ear. He said that they were with the police, then waited for an answer before hanging up.
