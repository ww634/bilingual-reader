# Writing project references

## The Last Clear Voice

The user selected and approved the core premise: older, grumpy, widowed audio specialist Daniel Mercer follows clues in a missing witness's voicemail to a phone box, then meets her adult son. Their difficult, funny, affectionate partnership develops alongside the investigation of Daniel's old case.

Read in this order before writing:

0. `the-last-clear-voice-french-style.md` — mandatory French register, narrative tenses, character voices, and learner alignment rules.
1. `crime-thriller-writing-guide.md` — standing craft principles and benchmark novels.
2. `the-last-clear-voice-story-bible.md` — current working canon, complete crime solution, chronology, characters, evidence custody, research gates.
   Read `the-last-clear-voice-continuity-through-ch7.md` alongside the bible for published timings, geography, source handling, permissions and relationship details before continuing with chapter 8.
   Then read `the-last-clear-voice-continuity-through-ch12.md` for chapters 8–12, including the exact planted wording, document provenance, new characters and chapter 13 handoff.
3. `the-last-clear-voice-chapter-plan.md` — chapter objectives, scenes, arcs, pacing, dependencies, and endings.
4. `the-last-clear-voice-clue-ledger.md` — clue plants, transformations, red herrings, fair-play and rereading audit.
5. `the-last-clear-voice-series-layer.md` — unknown adviser, subtle secondary seeds, Ruth’s late death, and future partnership.

The approved core is locked. The detailed solution and outline are the assistant's working implementation, open to deliberate user revision. Exact audio samples and jurisdiction-specific procedures remain subject to verification; preserve each clue's narrative function if a technical implementation changes.

Earlier proposals and `the-last-clear-voice-story-foundation.md` are historical, not current canon. Spoilers are authorised. Do not change files for the Chinese book as part of this project.
